# Competitive research (September 2026)

| Product | What stood out |
|---|---|
| [Tentacle](https://usetentacle.app/) | Weekly digest of blogs; public [/open](https://usetentacle.app/open) metrics (243 users, 633 blogs, $17/mo costs) |
| Blogtrottr, Feedrabbit, follow.it | One email per post, not a digest. Complaints: ads, badly scaled images, interstitial clicks. ~$19–25/year |
| [Digest](https://usedigest.com/), Mailbrew | Many sources, AI summaries, flexible schedules, newsletter inbox |
| [Feedbin](https://feedbin.com/) ($70/yr), Inoreader, Feedly | OPML, full-text extraction, keyword rules / mute filters, newsletter inbox, page change tracking |
| Discovery | ooh.directory (~2,400 blogs), Kagi Small Web, blogrolls |

**Conclusions:** Tidepool already matches Tentacle. The gap is what paid
digest services and readers have. Next, in order: OPML import plus starter
packs, the newsletter inbox, then AI summaries and delivery frequency. Skip
X/Reddit sources. The roadmap lives in [[Tracker.base|the tracker]].
